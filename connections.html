02how it works

no swiping. no profiles. someone you already stood next to.

  1. 01

    You fill out a card

    Pick someone specific and describe them: what they wore, where you were standing, about what time. If you got their Instagram, add it. Or pick set me up and tell us who you're interested in.

  2. 02

    We read it ourselves

    Every card goes to the treehouse. team and nobody else. Nothing is posted and there's no public list to scroll through.

  3. 03

    We check if it's mutual

    Mutual means you both named each other. Before any intro we message each of you separately to confirm, so nobody gets surprised.

  4. 04

    We introduce you

    If it's a yes on both sides, we put you in touch on Instagram and step out of the way. If it isn't, nothing happens and they never know you asked.

05questions

the fine print.

Who sees my card?

The treehouse. team. It isn't posted anywhere and there's no public board. If you named someone, they only hear about it if they named you too.

What if it isn't mutual?

Nothing happens. They never find out you asked, and your card stays with us.

I didn't get their name. Is that a problem?

No. Describe them as well as you can: what they wore, where you were standing, about what time, who they were with. The more detail, the better our odds of working out who you mean.

What does "set me up" mean?

You don't have one person in mind, you're just open. We keep your card and reach out if there's someone from the room we think you should meet.

How will I hear from you?

On Instagram, from @treehouseevents.nyc. That's why the card asks for your handle. If you don't hear from us, it wasn't mutual this time.

Who is this for?

Anyone who's been to a treehouse. party, is single and is 21 or older. You tell us who you're interested in, and it's open to everyone.

I haven't been to a party yet.

Then start there. Get on the list and come to the next one.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Message @treehouseevents.nyc and we'll take your card out.
